**note_types**: cards of these note types get the single button. "Task" is created for you.

**button_label**: text on the button.

**skip_show_answer**: if true, the card goes straight to the Complete button with no "Show Answer" step.

**ease_first_time**: how Complete is recorded the first time you see a card. 4 = Easy (the card leaves the learning queue at once), 3 = Good (with default settings it comes back in about 10 minutes).

**ease_review**: how Complete is recorded on later reviews. 3 = Good, 4 = Easy (intervals grow faster), 2 = Hard (slower).
